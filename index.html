FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick Answers to Common Questions

How do I track my shipment?
Enter your Consignment Number / AWB or Customer Reference ID into our tracking widget above to get real-time milestone updates.
What is the delivery time for different services?
Express Air delivers within 24-48 hours across major metros, while Surface Cargo takes 3-5 business days depending on distance.
What if I am not available at the time of delivery?
Our delivery executive will re-attempt delivery on the next business day or coordinate via phone call/SMS.
Do you provide insurance for shipments?
Yes, transit risk insurance coverage is available for high-value corporate and commercial cargo upon booking request.
